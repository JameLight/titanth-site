Who it is for

Stores outside the EU that ship to EU consumers themselves — typically through FedEx, UPS, DHL Express or another carrier that asks for product identifiers on the commercial invoice — and that keep their catalog in Shopify.

If your checkout or logistics provider (for example Zonos, Passport or a fulfilment platform) already collects product identifiers for you, check there first; you may not need this tool.

Limits, stated plainly

  • It cannot know a supplier's code you have not entered. Standard Shopify exports contain none; the tool helps you collect and map them.
  • It checks barcode format and reuse, not ownership. A valid-looking barcode can still be wrong.
  • It is not legal advice and does not file anything with customs.
