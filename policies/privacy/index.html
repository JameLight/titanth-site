Privacy notice

The checker and the product. The free checker and TITANTH Catalog Check run inside your browser. Your CSV is not uploaded to us and no usage data is sent from the tool.

This website. Static pages. Analytics: this site uses GoatCounter (titanth.goatcounter.com), a privacy-focused service that sets no cookies and stores no personal data, to count page views and link clicks to the checker or the buy page. Some pages distinguish clicks to the synthetic sample from clicks to check a visitor's own CSV. These events record an intent to open the checker, not whether a check was completed. Analytics does not run inside the free checker or the purchased tool. GoatCounter records the page, referrer, browser type, screen size and country derived from a truncated IP address. Nothing typed into the free checker is sent anywhere.

Checkout. Purchases are processed by Shopify, with payment by Stripe (cards) or PayPal. They receive your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policies; the store's own privacy policy, generated by Shopify, is at shop.titanth.com/policies/privacy-policy. We receive your order details (name, email, order number) to deliver the product and provide support, and keep them as long as needed for accounting and support.

Email. If you write to jamelight@titanth.com we keep the correspondence to answer you.

Your rights. Ask us at jamelight@titanth.com to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you.
