How to export from Shopify: Products → Export → "All products" → "CSV for Excel, Numbers, or other spreadsheet programs" → Export. Open the checker, choose the downloaded file.

What it finds

  • Rows with no SKU (your product code)
  • Barcodes that are blank, not a valid GTIN/EAN/UPC, look like placeholders (e.g. N/A), or are reused on another product
  • Shopify's multi-barcode Barcodes column (new in September 2026, e.g. gtin:00012345678905; upc:012345678905; INTERNAL-123): the GTIN/EAN/UPC/ISBN entry is used as the standardised identifier; ASIN and internal codes are listed but not counted as one
  • Rows with no maker's / supplier's code — which is almost every row in a standard export
  • Product-level values such as Vendor carried down to each variant so supplier questions are grouped correctly

What it does not do

It does not invent codes, contact suppliers, verify that a barcode belongs to your product, file a customs declaration, or certify compliance. It shows you where the data gaps are and what to do about each one.

Questions

Does my product data get uploaded?

No. The checker runs entirely inside your browser tab. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Which file do I use?

Shopify admin → Products → Export → CSV for Excel, Numbers or other spreadsheet programs. Use the standard export; you do not need to edit it.

Why is every row 'missing a maker's code'?

Standard Shopify exports have no column for the manufacturer's or supplier's own product code. That is the code EU customs calls the non-standardised manufacturer product identifier. You need to collect it from suppliers and add a column; the checker lets you map that column when you have it.

What does the paid version add?

Your whole catalog (up to 10,000 rows), supplier question sheets grouped by vendor, a record of checked corrections, and exports. US$59 once, for one store.
