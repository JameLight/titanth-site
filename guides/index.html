Y081 explained

Y081 is the code a declarant enters to state that no standardised product identifier (GTIN/EAN/UPC) exists for an item. It does not replace the merchant or manufacturer code.

Handmade: tolerance list

POD, personalised, second-hand

The Commission guidance says customised, personalised, print-on-demand, second-hand and refurbished goods get no special treatment: declare the product identifiers of the underlying or original product. Examples for POD apparel, engraved gifts and resold items.

New customs code (2026/2108)

Regulation (EU) 2026/2108, the new Union Customs Code, was published on 19 September 2026. Most of it applies from 21 September 2027; a per-item Union handling fee on distance-sale goods starts once its amount is set (Parliament: at the latest 1 November 2026); the EU Customs Data Hub takes over e-commerce data from 1 July 2028. The product identifier date, 1 November 2026, does not move.
