1 November 2026: the EU product identifier deadline

1 July 2026 — identifiers may be declared voluntarily; no penalties for missing data. 1 November 2026 — required on customs declarations for consumer parcels imported into the EU. After that date, declarations without the required identifiers can be rejected and parcels held.

Questions

Will parcels shipped before 1 November but arriving after be affected?

The requirement attaches to the customs declaration. Ask your carrier how they treat parcels in transit around the date; do not rely on ship date alone.

Is the €3 duty the same thing?

No. The €3 per item duty on low-value consumer imports started 1 July 2026 and is about money; product identifiers are about data on the declaration. They arrived in the same reform package.
