EU product identifiers by platform: Etsy, eBay, WooCommerce, Shopify

EU product-identifier data rules concern certain distance sales of imported goods. A listing cannot tell you whether a particular order falls within the rules or who supplies the customs data. Check the shipment and declaration route with your marketplace, carrier or customs representative before treating a blank catalog field as a compliance failure.

The shared problem

A platform may hold some identifiers, but its listing cannot verify a supplier's reference or show how it reaches the person filing the declaration. For resold goods, ask the supplier for the model reference and its source, then confirm where your marketplace or carrier expects it. Use the supplier request template.

Questions

I sell on a marketplace that handles customs — do I still need this?

Check who files the declaration and whether that service collects merchant and manufacturer identifiers for each item. If you do not know, ask the marketplace's logistics partner or carrier. Listing fields alone cannot answer this.

Handmade goods without barcodes?

If you make the item, keep the product or model reference you actually assign and its source. A missing barcode is not by itself a customs-code decision; ask the person lodging the declaration which information or exception applies.
