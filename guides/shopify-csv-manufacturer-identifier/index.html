Where is the manufacturer product identifier in a Shopify export?

Short answer: it is not there. The standard Shopify product CSV has Variant SKU (your code) and Variant Barcode (the retail barcode), but no field for the code the maker or supplier uses for the item. EU customs calls that the non-standardised manufacturer product identifier and, from 1 November 2026, expects it on consumer parcels.

The three codes, mapped to Shopify

EU customs termPlain meaningShopify column
Merchant product identifier (M-PID)Your own code for the itemVariant SKU (or the variant ID)
Non-standardised manufacturer product identifier (NS-PID)The maker's or supplier's own model / part / article numberNone. Google Shopping / MPN exists but is product-level and rarely filled
Standardised manufacturer product identifier (S-PID)GTIN / EAN / UPC / ISBN — the retail barcodeVariant Barcode

What to do

  1. Decide where the maker's code will live. Options: a variant metafield, the Google Shopping / MPN column (product-level only — fine if all variants share one model number), or a separate spreadsheet you keep next to the export.
  2. Collect the codes from suppliers. Ask for the model / part / article number for each variant, not just the product. Use this email template.
  3. Do not substitute. A barcode is not the maker's code; the vendor name is not the maker's code; your SKU is not the maker's code. Customs treats them as three different fields.
  4. Check what you already have. Run your export through the free checker to see blank SKUs and bad barcodes while you wait for suppliers.
If you make the goods yourself, you are the manufacturer: your own model number is the maker's code, and you may use the same value as the SKU if that is genuinely your model reference.

Questions

Can I use the barcode as the manufacturer identifier?

No. The barcode is the standardised identifier (S-PID). The non-standardised manufacturer identifier is a separate field for the maker's own model or part number.

What if the supplier says they have no code?

Ask for whatever they use internally on invoices or packing lists — a model, article or part number. If there is truly none, record that answer; the rule expects a code from the manufacturer, producer or supplier, and your carrier or customs representative can advise on that case.

Does Google Shopping / MPN work?

It can hold a model number, but it is product-level in the Shopify export, so variants that need different codes cannot be distinguished. Use it only when all variants share the same manufacturer model number.
