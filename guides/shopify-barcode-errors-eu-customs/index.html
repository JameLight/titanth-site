Barcode errors in Shopify exports that will fail at EU customs

The standardised identifier is the one field most stores already have — and it is often wrong in ways that are easy to miss in a spreadsheet.

The five errors we see

ErrorWhat it looks likeFix
Wrong length11 or 15 digitsGTINs are 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits. Re-copy from packaging.
Bad check digit13 digits that do not validateOne digit was mistyped. Re-copy; do not guess.

Questions

Is a valid check digit proof the barcode is correct?

No. It proves the digits are consistent, not that the number belongs to your product. Confirm ownership with the supplier or the packaging.

Can I make up an internal barcode?

Not for customs. The standardised identifier must come from a recognised standard (GS1 GTIN/EAN/UPC, ISBN). An internal number belongs in the SKU field.
