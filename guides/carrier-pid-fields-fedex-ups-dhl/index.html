Where carriers take the EU product identifiers

The regulation says what customs needs; each carrier decides where you type it. The pattern is the same everywhere: identifiers go on the commercial invoice line items or the equivalent item fields in the shipping platform or API — and every carrier expects you to bring the values.

What each carrier's own pages say (read 22 September 2026)

FedExUK customs page: product identifiers are required for every product in a B2C shipment regardless of value (not for B2B VAT-registered recipients) and enforced from 1 November 2026. FedEx Ship Manager on fedex.com, the FedEx API and the Import Tool take the codes per line item. Installed Ship Manager, Ship Manager Server and Unified Specification Exchange support them only from March 2027 — until then FedEx says to add the codes to the commercial invoice or packing list. UPSUS customs page: three product identifiers required per line item for B2C shipments as of 1 November (B2B: not required). UPS's own checklist: identify affected products, collect the merchant, manufacturer and standardised identifiers, validate data quality, update systems, test — "missing or inaccurate data could lead to customs delays or holds".Work the checklist against your Shopify export. Field names for WorldShip and the API were not on that page — ask your UPS contact.
DHL ExpressUK page (guidance as of June 2026): a second wave of mandatory data on 1 November 2026 for all B2C import declarations; the merchant identifier and the manufacturer identifier must be included, the standardised identifier is described as optional. Codes are entered per product line in MyDHL+ or added to your integration's shipment data.Enter all three where you can. DHL's wording for the manufacturer identifier ("yours / the manufacturer's internal product identifier, such as a SKU") is looser than the regulation, which assigns it to the manufacturer, producer or supplier — see Same SKU for both?

Prepare the data once, use it everywhere

Whatever the carrier calls the fields, the three values per item are the same. Get them right in your catalog first — check your Shopify export — then map them into each carrier's fields.

Questions

Do I put the codes on the customs form or the invoice?

For express carriers the commercial invoice line items (or the equivalent item fields in their software) are where the identifiers are captured; the carrier's broker moves them into the declaration. FedEx says explicitly that shippers whose tools cannot yet carry the codes should add them to the commercial invoice or packing list.

What if I use a shipping app connected to Shopify?

Check whether the app has item-level fields for the three identifiers and whether it reads them from Shopify. Sendcloud documents three fields; Shippo's API docs showed none on 22 September 2026. Most apps read SKU and barcode; few have a manufacturer-code field yet, so you may need to add it in the app or on the invoice.

Do B2B shipments need product identifiers?

FedEx and UPS both state the requirement is for B2C shipments and does not apply to B2B VAT-registered recipients in the destination country. Confirm your case with your customs representative.
