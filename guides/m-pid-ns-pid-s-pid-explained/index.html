M-PID, NS-PID and S-PID — what they mean for an online seller

EU customs rules for consumer parcels use three product identifiers. The names are clumsy; the idea is simple: who is selling it, who made it, and what universal barcode does it carry.

TermPlain meaningExampleRequired?
M-PID — merchant product identifierThe seller's or marketplace's own code for the listingSKU TW-BGVL3-10, or a listing / variant IDYes
NS-PID — non-standardised manufacturer product identifierThe maker's, producer's or supplier's own reference, not based on a global standardModel BGVL3, part number A-1042-BLKYes
S-PID — standardised manufacturer product identifierA code based on an international standardEAN-13 8858812345671, UPC, ISBNWhere one exists

Y081

Y081 is the code a declarant uses to state that no standardised identifier exists for the item. It does not excuse a missing manufacturer code; it only covers the barcode field. A hand-made item with no barcode can be declared with Y081 — it still needs the merchant code and a maker's code.

Common mistakes

  • Putting the barcode in the manufacturer-code field. They are different fields.
  • Using the vendor's name as the manufacturer code. It must be an item reference, not a company name.
  • Reusing one barcode across different products. A barcode identifies one exact item.
  • Leaving the SKU blank on some variants. Every shipped variant needs its own merchant code.

Definitions are in Article 1 of Regulation (EU) 2026/1022. This page is an explanation, not legal advice.

Questions

Can the SKU and the manufacturer code be the same value?

Only if you are the manufacturer and your SKU is genuinely your model reference. For resold goods they are different codes from different parties.

Is an Amazon ASIN an M-PID?

A marketplace listing identifier can serve as the merchant product identifier for sales on that marketplace. For your own store, use your SKU or variant reference.

Do variants need separate codes?

Yes for the merchant code and, where the maker distinguishes them, for the manufacturer code and barcode. A size or colour with its own barcode has its own identifiers.
