Find the product identifiers EU customs will ask for — before a parcel gets held.

From 1 November 2026, customs declarations for goods sold at a distance to EU consumers and imported into the EU must carry three product identifiers: your own product code, the maker's or supplier's code, and the retail barcode where one exists (Regulation (EU) 2026/1022; Commission guidance §3.5). Standard Shopify exports have no column for the maker's code. TITANTH shows you which rows need work, what to ask your supplier, and keeps a record of what you fixed.
