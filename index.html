Why product identifiers matter for EU shipments

For the distance-sale imports covered by the rule, the customs data calls for a merchant product identifier, a maker or supplier identifier, and a standardised identifier where one exists. Scope and exceptions need case-by-case review.

CodeCustoms nameWhere it usually lives in ShopifyRequired?
Your product codeMerchant product identifier (M-PID)Variant SKUYes
Maker's / supplier's codeNon-standardised manufacturer product identifier (NS-PID)No standard column — you must collect itYes
BarcodeStandardised manufacturer product identifier (S-PID: GTIN, EAN, UPC, ISBN)Barcodes (or Variant Barcode in older exports)Where one exists

Source: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1022. Voluntary from 1 July 2026, mandatory from 1 November 2026. This page is a data-readiness aid, not legal advice.
